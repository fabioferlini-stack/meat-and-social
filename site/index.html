The butchery

Quality over
compromise.

At the core of Meat & Social is Bartosz Frankowski, the Master Butcher whose decades of experience shape everything we serve, from whole-animal butchery to dry-ageing.

We source from trusted, sustainable, small-scale farms, and every cut is prepared on site. The counter is open for you too: come by, see what's on the block, chat to the team and take something special home. Halal cuts available.
